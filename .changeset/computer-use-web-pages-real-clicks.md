Computer Use clicks controls on web pages with the pointer from the start, in the background, instead of an accessibility press that pages such as Canva accept without doing anything: a Canva side tab now switches on the first click (about 0.75 s instead of two tries). A followed link waits at most 3 s for its page instead of 8 s; a page that changes does so within half a second.
