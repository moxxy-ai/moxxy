Desktop browser, found by running the packaged app in its narrow Browser pane: a press goes to the middle of an element's largest line box, so a link that wraps onto two lines is pressed on its text, not between the lines. `browser_snapshot` with `full: true` always sends the whole tree, never "unchanged". A row the snapshot collapses past its depth cap keeps the text it holds, so prices deep in a product list reach the agent.
