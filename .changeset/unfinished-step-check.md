A turn that ends right on a step that did not get done is reminded once to take another way or to say what stops it. A run of browser or Computer Use steps that stops short now says so in its result (`shortfall`), and the default mode asks once before the turn closes — not after a later step that worked, a hand-over to the user, a refused permission, an error or Stop. Switch it off with `context.unfinishedStepCheck: false`.
