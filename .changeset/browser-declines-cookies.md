Moxxy Browser now turns down a cookie banner itself when the banner shows a way to decline what the site does not need ("Reject all", "Only necessary", "Tylko niezbędne", "Odrzuć opcjonalne") and carries on with the task. Before, every cookie banner stopped the agent and was handed to you. It still never presses a control that accepts, and a banner with no way to decline is still yours to answer.
