Read aloud and Voice Mode read a table row by row, each value with the name of its column ("Claude: Wynik 9 z 9."), instead of all its cells in one run. A web address is no longer read out or broken into pieces at its dots: a link is said as a link to its site ("link do strony github"), a bare domain as the site's name, and a link with its own text by that text.
