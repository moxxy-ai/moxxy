The work panel on the right (terminal, files, diff and browser) no longer leaves a strip of icons when it is closed. Closed, it is gone and the conversation has the full width. Open it with the new button in the run's header or with ⌘J / Ctrl+J; it comes back on the pane you used last. It still opens by itself when the agent starts using its browser or terminal.
