The desktop app starts its move to a messenger look, where the conversation is the main thing on screen. This first step changes the foundation only: neutral greys instead of the blue-green panel, the system text face instead of a monospaced one, and rounder corners, in both the light and the dark theme. Buttons and entrances also move on sharper curves, and nothing bounces. The orange accent stays. The layout is unchanged for now; the sidebar, header, message bubbles and composer follow in later steps. The mobile app keeps its current look.
