Voice Mode's microphone button now sits in the closed voice card, beside what Moxxy is doing ("Listening", "Speaking", "Thinking"…): in the accent while it listens, cut and red while it is off. Before, it was hidden on the right of the card until the card opened, so with the card closed you could tell the microphone was off only while Moxxy was idle. It stays in that one place whether the microphone is on or off and as the card opens; the right of the card keeps only End. A screen reader hears "…, microphone off" after the state.
