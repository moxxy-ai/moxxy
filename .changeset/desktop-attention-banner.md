A chat you are not reading now calls you back in two more cases: when it stops to ask for a decision, and when the chat on screen finishes while Moxxy is in the background. While Moxxy is in the background it also shows a system notification with the chat's name and the first line of the answer; clicking it opens that chat. The sound and the notification each have a switch in Settings → Preferences → Notifications.
